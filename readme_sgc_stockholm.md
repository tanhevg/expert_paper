This dataset contains the results of protein expression and small scale purification experiments performed at SGC Stockholm between 2005 and 2013. 11,023 constructs were expressed in _Escherichia coli_ and purified with Ni-NTA IMAC from 1ml culture. Yields were estimated manually, based on relative thickness of SDS-PAGE gel bands. The results are formatted according to EXPERT templates.
