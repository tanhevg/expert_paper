This dataset contains the results of expressing and purifying human secreted proteins (human secretome) in mammalian cells. Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) and Human Embryo Kidney (HEK) were used for protein expression. Protein purification was performed on multiple scales, from millilitres to litres. The dataset contains 2,167 unique constructs. The results are formatted according to EXPERT templates.
